## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Backend Developer** with around 2 years of hands-on experience building web applications and REST APIs with **Laravel (PHP)** and **NestJS (TypeScript)**.

I enjoy designing backend systems that are:

* Clean and maintainable
* Secure and well-structured
* Scalable and performance-oriented
* Easy to integrate with other services

My experience includes **API design, authentication, authorization, database architecture, payment integrations, third-party APIs, caching, asynchronous processing, Docker, testing, and observability**.

I'm currently pursuing a **Bachelor's degree in Information Technology Engineering at Damascus University**.
